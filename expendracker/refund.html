Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated 4 November 2026

What you can buy

Expendracker is free to download and use. Two items are paid, each a one-time in-app purchase (inclusive of applicable taxes), delivered immediately as a digital feature:

  • Remove ads, ₹99: removes the ad on the app's home screen for good.
  • Backup, ₹49, from version 1.2.1: backs up your records to your own Google Drive. Free for anyone who turned on backup before version 1.2.1.

There are no subscriptions or recurring charges.

Cancellation

Because there is no subscription, there is nothing to cancel after purchase. You can cancel a purchase before completing it by closing the Google Play payment screen. If a payment is still pending, it is cancelled automatically by Google Play if it does not complete.

Refunds

  1. Within 48 hours of purchase: request a refund directly from Google Play at play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory, choose the order, then Request a refund. Google decides these requests under its own policy.
  2. Within 7 days of purchase: email us at vigneshj8133@gmail.com with your Google Play order number (it starts with GPA.) and the reason. If the feature you bought does not work for you and we cannot fix it, we refund the full amount.
  3. If a feature you bought stops working within 12 months of your purchase, on the Google Play account that bought it, and we cannot fix it, we refund the full amount on request.

Approved refunds are issued through Google Play to your original payment method. Google Play usually processes them within 3 to 5 business days, although your bank may take longer to show the credit. Once refunded, the feature is taken off your account: after a remove ads refund the ad shows again, and after a backup refund automatic backup stops. Your records on your phone and any backup file already in your Drive are not deleted.
